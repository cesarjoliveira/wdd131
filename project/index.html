Welcome to Alecrim Children's Orphanage

The Orphanage is a safe and welcoming refuge for children who have been victims of abuse and neglect in their homes. We understand that every child deserves an environment that promotes their emotional, physical, and social development, free from trauma and insecurity. Our mission is to provide not only shelter but also love, care, and support, allowing each child to rediscover their dignity and hope for a better future.
