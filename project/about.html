About Alecrim Children's Orphanage

Alecrim Children's Orphanage is dedicated to providing a safe, nurturing environment for children who have faced abuse and neglect. Our mission is to foster emotional, physical, and social development in a loving atmosphere.

Our Mission

We believe that every child deserves a chance at a brighter future. Our goal is to create a supportive community where children can learn, grow, and thrive.

Our Vision

To transform the lives of children in need by providing them with the resources, skills, and love necessary to become self-sufficient and productive members of society.

Our Values

  • Compassion: We care deeply about the well-being of each child.
  • Integrity: We act with honesty and transparency in all our dealings.
  • Respect: We honor the dignity of every child and family.
  • Collaboration: We work together with families, communities, and partners to achieve our mission.
